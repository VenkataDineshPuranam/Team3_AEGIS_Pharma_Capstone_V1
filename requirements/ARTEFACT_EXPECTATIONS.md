# Mandatory Participant Artefacts

All artefacts must be completed by participants and stored under `submission/`. Templates are blank scaffolds, not answers.

## Discovery and value

- Problem statement, current-state baseline, KPI tree and measurable value hypothesis.
- No-AI, rules, analytics, workflow-redesign and buy/build/partner comparison.
- Stakeholder map, incentives, decision rights, conflicts and escalation design.
- DMAIC analysis, benefits-realisation plan and stop/pivot criteria.

## Product and operating model

- Personas, jobs-to-be-done, service blueprint, intended use and prohibited uses.
- Human oversight, review, override, contestability and emergency-stop design.
- Adoption, training, accessibility, multilingual and change-management plan.

## Domain, data and knowledge

- DDD bounded contexts and context map across Research, Clinical, Manufacturing, Quality, Safety, Regulatory and Supply.
- Data inventory, contracts, quality, lineage, provenance, retention, residency and stewardship.
- Pharmaceutical ontology and semantic layer with temporal and jurisdictional semantics.
- Defensible knowledge-graph decision, including a simpler alternative benchmark.
- Document authority, effective-date, supersession and conflict-resolution model.

## Architecture and engineering

- Traceable functional, non-functional, GxP, safety, privacy and security requirements.
- C4 architecture, integration contracts, event semantics and at least ten meaningful ADRs.
- Brownfield modernization, migration, coexistence, cutover, rollback and decommissioning plan.
- Reproducible offline execution, locked dependencies, reset, seeded data and evidence export.
- Cursor-oriented engineering evidence: rules, skills, agents, commands, prompts and review controls.

## AI and agentic design

- Model and non-model baselines, model-selection evidence and substitution strategy.
- Prompt, retrieval, schema, tool, state, memory and context-boundary design.
- Budgets, stop conditions, checkpointing, idempotency and bounded human-approved tools.
- Deterministic test mode, safe degradation, kill switch and AI-disabled continuity.

## GxP, safety and assurance

- Intended-purpose and regulatory-applicability analysis.
- Computerised-system lifecycle and validation/assurance strategy proportionate to risk.
- Data-integrity assessment, electronic-record/signature boundary and audit-trail controls.
- Pharmaceutical quality risk management and clinical/safety assurance case.
- EU AI Act and ISO/IEC 42001-aligned governance analysis where applicable.
- Claims-arguments-evidence assurance case with invalidation conditions and residual risk.

## Security and privacy

- Threat and abuse model covering injection, poisoning, exfiltration, tool abuse, excessive agency, replay, supply chain and denial-of-wallet.
- Zero Trust authorization, signed tools, least privilege and segregation of duties.
- Privacy-by-design assessment covering consent, secondary use, pseudonymisation, re-identification and cross-border processing.
- Red-team evidence, remediation verification and residual-risk acceptance.

## Evaluation, economics and operations

- TEVV plan, datasets, graders, thresholds, subgroup analysis and regression gates.
- Token efficiency, context budgets, caching, avoided inference and token-economics evidence.
- Full AI FinOps model including human review, validation, security, observability and exit cost.
- SLI/SLO, error budget, capacity, observability, incident response, backup, restore and disaster recovery.
- Vendor exit, model replacement, data portability, retirement and evidence-preservation strategy.
- Production-readiness gaps, 90-day roadmap, handover pack and final defence.

## Working POC

The POC must implement the three mandatory workflows with happy, edge, failure, attack, recovery and manual-fallback paths. It must not perform a prohibited regulated action.
