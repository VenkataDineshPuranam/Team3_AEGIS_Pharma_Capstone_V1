# Final Defence

The defence must include:

1. A 60-second elevator pitch and five-minute executive case.
2. Current-state baseline, no-AI comparison and measurable value case.
3. Live demonstration of all three workflows.
4. A prohibited-action test proving the system cannot cross regulated boundaries.
5. A malicious-document and poisoned-tool challenge.
6. Unit, terminology, temporal and product-identity conflict handling.
7. Model outage, fallback limitation and AI-disabled manual operation.
8. Evaluation evidence, subgroup weaknesses and failed-gate behaviour.
9. Token and total-cost defence including human review.
10. Architecture, DDD, ontology, semantic layer, KG decision and ADR defence.
11. Inspection-style evidence request linking claims to immutable artefacts.
12. Vendor exit, model substitution and retirement demonstration.
13. A board recommendation: go, conditional go, pivot, pause or stop.

The pitch must state the problem, affected decisions, bounded AI intervention, measurable value, strongest safety/control boundary and the next decision required. No finished pitch is provided.
