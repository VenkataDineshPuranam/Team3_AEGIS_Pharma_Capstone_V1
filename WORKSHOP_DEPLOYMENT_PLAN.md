# Workshop Deployment Plan — 40 Hours

This plan can run as five full days, ten four-hour sessions, or a distributed capstone. All injects are available from the beginning.

| Stage | Hours | Participant outcome | Exit evidence |
|---|---:|---|---|
| 0. Preflight and orientation | 2 | Package runs offline; team understands boundaries and scoring | Preflight report, team charter, working agreements |
| 1. Discovery and DMAIC | 5 | Problem, baseline, stakeholders, no-AI comparison and value hypothesis | Artefacts 01–04, assumptions and decision log |
| 2. Domain and evidence model | 5 | Bounded contexts, source authority, data contracts, ontology/semantic/KG decision | Artefacts 05–09, evidence map |
| 3. Architecture and controls | 6 | C4, ADRs, integration contracts, GxP lifecycle, quality risk | Artefacts 10–15, contract tests |
| 4. Secure AI/agent design | 5 | Bounded inference/tools, threat model, privacy, human factors and governance | Artefacts 16–21, prohibited-action tests |
| 5. POC build | 8 | Three workflows operate in deterministic offline mode | Code, fixtures, tests, evidence export |
| 6. TEVV, FinOps and reliability | 4 | Release gates, subgroup evidence, cost/token measures, outage and recovery | Artefacts 22–25, machine-readable results |
| 7. Operating model and transfer | 3 | Production readiness, operating model, vendor exit and roadmap | Artefacts 26–29, runbooks |
| 8. Defence | 2 | Executive and technical defence with live failure demonstrations | Artefact 30, final recommendation |

## Recommended team roles

Product/value lead, domain and evidence lead, architecture/integration lead, GxP and quality lead, security/privacy lead, evaluation/reliability lead, and build lead. One person may hold multiple roles, but decision rights and independent review must remain visible.

## Suggested checkpoints

- Hour 7: problem and no-AI qualification review.
- Hour 12: authority, identity, time, unit and lineage review.
- Hour 18: architecture and prohibited-action contract review.
- Hour 26: working three-workflow demonstration.
- Hour 34: red-team, outage and cost gate review.
- Hour 38: clean-room handover rehearsal.

## Facilitator-independent rule

Teams must not depend on hidden answer keys, private credentials, later injects or undocumented oral instructions. Any assumption required to proceed must be recorded in the assumptions and decision logs.
