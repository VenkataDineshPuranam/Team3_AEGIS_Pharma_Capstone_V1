# Reference Sources

Start with `LOCAL_REGULATORY_AND_STANDARDS_GUIDE.md`; it is included for offline workshop use. The links below are optional research anchors and are not required to execute the capstone. Participants must verify current jurisdictional applicability before making legal or regulatory claims.


These sources inform the challenge boundaries but do not provide a case solution.

- US FDA — Computer Software Assurance for Production and Quality Management System Software: https://www.fda.gov/regulatory-information/search-fda-guidance-documents/computer-software-assurance-production-and-quality-management-system-software
- European Commission — EudraLex Volume 4 GMP: https://health.ec.europa.eu/medicinal-products/eudralex/eudralex-volume-4_en
- European Commission — Annex 11 Computerised Systems and Annex 15 Qualification and Validation.
- EMA — ICH E6(R3) Good Clinical Practice: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/ich-e6-good-clinical-practice-scientific-guideline
- ICH — Q9(R1) Quality Risk Management: https://www.ich.org/page/quality-guidelines
- EMA — Good Pharmacovigilance Practices: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/post-authorisation/pharmacovigilance-post-authorisation/good-pharmacovigilance-practices-gvp
- EMA — ISO IDMP and SPOR master data: https://www.ema.europa.eu/en/human-regulatory-overview/research-development/data-medicines-iso-idmp-standards-overview
- CDSCO — New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019 and official updates: https://www.cdsco.gov.in/opencms/opencms/en/Acts-and-rules/New-Drugs/
- WHO — pharmaceutical production, data integrity and GMP guidance: https://www.who.int/teams/health-product-and-policy-standards/standards-and-specifications/norms-and-standards-for-pharmaceuticals/guidelines/production

Participants must verify current applicability and jurisdictional status before making legal or regulatory claims.
