# Local Regulatory and Standards Guide

This concise guide makes the capstone independent of internet access. It is a training summary, not legal advice or a substitute for controlled copies of laws, standards or guidance.

## Electronic records and GxP computerised systems

Participants should determine whether a component creates, modifies, maintains, archives, retrieves or transmits regulated records; whether electronic signatures are used; and what controls are needed for access, audit trails, record retention, backup, change control and trustworthy evidence. Risk-based assurance does not remove accountability for intended use, requirements, testing, data integrity or controlled operation.

## Pharmaceutical quality and manufacturing

Quality risk management should connect hazards, detectability, severity, uncertainty, controls, residual risk and review. Batch disposition and certification remain with authorized Quality roles. AI may reconcile evidence and expose contradictions but must not silently convert units, overwrite original results, close deviations, determine OOS invalidity, release product or initiate recall.

## Clinical development

Clinical systems and AI support must preserve protocol/site/version applicability, participant rights, consent and withdrawal, blinding, source evidence, auditability, data corrections and accountable medical or investigator decisions. Eligibility and endpoint decisions remain human and governed.

## Pharmacovigilance

PV support must preserve verbatim information, source and receipt evidence, terminology version, duplicate uncertainty, listedness context and qualified medical review. Automated irreversible merging, final seriousness/causality/expectedness/reportability decisions, or automatic authority submission are outside the permitted scope.

## Privacy and cross-border use

State purpose, lawful basis or permission assumptions, data categories, minimisation, retention, access, re-identification risk, consent/withdrawal propagation, secondary-use boundary, residency and transfer controls. A deletion request may require restriction, segregation or retention rather than simplistic deletion where legal, trial-integrity or GxP obligations apply.

## AI governance and security

Define intended use, prohibited use, accountable owner, risk tier, lifecycle controls, evaluation, incident response, supplier management, change control and retirement. Apply least privilege, continuous authorization, signed tools, segregation of duties, prompt/document isolation, provenance, bounded steps, budgets, idempotency and fail-closed execution.

## Applicability method

For each claim, document: jurisdiction; product/process; intended purpose; advisory versus determinative role; human accountability; regulated-record boundary; affected decision; applicable date/version; evidence source; assumption; and residual uncertainty.
