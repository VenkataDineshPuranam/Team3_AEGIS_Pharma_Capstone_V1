# Regulatory and Standards Boundary Pack

This pack provides research anchors, not legal conclusions. Participants must determine applicability, intended purpose, system boundary and evidence obligations.

Consider at minimum:

- FDA requirements and guidance for electronic records and electronic signatures, including 21 CFR Part 11 scope and application.
- FDA expectations for current good manufacturing practice and risk-based assurance of production and quality-system software where relevant.
- EU GMP Volume 4, including Annex 11 Computerised Systems, Annex 15 Qualification and Validation, Annex 1 for sterile manufacture where applicable, and the developing boundary for AI in GMP environments.
- ICH Q9(R1) Quality Risk Management and ICH Q10 Pharmaceutical Quality System.
- ICH E6(R3) Good Clinical Practice and ICH E8(R1) general considerations for clinical studies.
- EMA Good Pharmacovigilance Practices, including case management, signal management and risk minimisation.
- ISO IDMP / EMA SPOR and product-master-data obligations.
- India New Drugs and Clinical Trials Rules, 2019, applicable amendments and CDSCO expectations.
- Applicable privacy, cross-border, cybersecurity and AI-governance requirements, including GDPR and EU AI Act analysis where the intended use falls within scope.
- ISO/IEC 42001-aligned AI management, ISO/IEC 27001-aligned security management and applicable business-continuity controls.

## Boundary questions

1. Is the component used to create, modify, maintain, archive, retrieve or transmit a regulated record?
2. Does it influence product quality, trial integrity, participant safety, pharmacovigilance, labeling, release or recall?
3. Is the output advisory, workflow-supporting, determinative or executed through tools?
4. Which human role remains accountable and what evidence must that role inspect?
5. What validation, assurance, change-control and record-retention approach is proportionate to risk?
6. What is prohibited even if technically possible?
