## Evidence for "is model accuracy the dominant bottleneck?"

**INTERPRETATION** (provisional — to be tested, not assumed): the evidence so far points *against* model accuracy as the dominant bottleneck. Every named risk above is a **control/integration/process** failure mode (poisoned input, stale authorization, unsigned tool, missing evaluation harness, vendor concentration) rather than a case of a well-controlled model producing a wrong answer. This is consistent with `no_ai_baselines.csv` showing `master_data_repair` (non-AI) at 38% estimated value vs `genai_assist` at 51% — the gap is not overwhelming, and control failures would erode any of the 51% regardless of model quality.
